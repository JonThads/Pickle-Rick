# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

*Pickle Rick*

## Business Background

There is no existing Pickleball Court Management System for Admins and a
Pickleball Reservation System for Users. Pickle Rick — the app — solves
both problems into one solution.

## Business Goals

Admins should be able to manage their courts and see the revenue and
profit for each court that they manage.

## Business Processes (High-Level)

- Court Management, including Cancellation of user bookings by the Admins
- Revenue Dashboards
- Login, Registration, and Account Deletion

## Potential Pain Points

- Manual corrections

## Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-01 | The system must have a Login and Registration Page | High |
| BR-02 | The Registration Page should have an option for Users and Admins | High |
| BR-03 | The system must record user bookings for a specific hour time slot of a specific day of each court by a specific admin | High |
| BR-04 | The system must have an option for manual admin approval or auto-approval, configurable per court | High |
| BR-05 | The system must have a list of all courts managed by a specific admin | High |
| BR-06 | The system must have a "My Bookings" page for each User to let the User know on what games they are booked to | High |
| BR-07 | The system must have a shop and inventory for each court, where users can add items to their cart, purchase in advance, and pick up purchased items at the court they ordered from. | Med |
| BR-08 | The system must let a Player or Admin permanently delete their own account, removing the data that belongs to them. | Med |

## Key Metrics

- Revenue and Profit per court from Bookings and Sales for past and current months
- Potential Revenue based on a month's current and advanced bookings
